Lechona (roasted pig)

Lechona is often eaten during large gatherings such as Christmas, big birthdays, or inaugurations. For this reason, the cook typically needs a whole pig and a lot of time (generally half a day of cooking) to prepare it. However, for the purposes of this webpage, we'll show you a way to prepare the dish for fewer people and inv less time.

In most cases it is also served with some sides like vegetables, arepas or fruits (plaintains)

Ingredients

  • 60 g cup pork fat
  • 4 garlic cloves
  • 1 teaspoon ground cumin
  • 4 green onions
  • 200 g cup peas
  • 300 g cooked white rice
  • 1 kg pork meat
  • 1 kg pork fatback skins
  • Salt
  • Pepper

Steps

  1. Mince the garlic cloves and chop the green onions
  2. Add the pork fat to a pan and wait until it melts
  3. Add the garlic and the green onions; and cook for 4 minutes
  4. In a large bowl, mix the pork meat, cooked rice, peas, ground cumin, salt and pepper
  5. Add the mixture from the pan to the bowl and stir it
  6. Cover and place the bowl in the refrigerator for 1 hour
  7. Rinse the pork fatback skin and pat it dry, then line a baking tray with aluminum foil and place the pork fatback on it.
  8. Preheat the oven to 245°C
  9. After the mixture in the fridge is ready (after one hour), take it out and place it on the pork fatback skin
  10. Roll up the pork fatback skin to wrap the mixture
  11. Use kitchen string to tie the roll
  12. Bake uncovered to brown the skin for 45 minutes, then cover with aluminum foil and cook another 45 minutes
  13. Remove the lechona, let it rest for 15 minutes, then portion it
  14. Enjoy your lechona
