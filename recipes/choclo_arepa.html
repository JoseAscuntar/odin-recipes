Choclo Arepa

Since I was a child I've loved corn (probably my favorite basic food), and fortunately in Colombia it can be found in several recipes ranging from soups to most of arepas. However, Choclo Arepas have a special place in my heart because I'm from the cradle of this dish and I can't help but love it.

Generally, an arepa is just a round and flat snack made from corn or dough. There are quite a lot of types of arepas, but Choclo Arepa is just better (completely unbiased opinion) because, unlike the rest, it has a somewhat sweet flavor and keeps the texture of the corn, doing a really good snack or even a great side for a breakfast

Ingredients

  • A choclo (sweet corn)
  • A cup of milk
  • 1 tablespoon of sugar (or panela)
  • 1 teaspoon of salt
  • 1.5 cups of colombian Chitaga cheese (or any stretchy fresh cheese)
  • 1 cup of Sabanero (or mozarella) cheese
  • 1 egg
  • Butter (for greasing the hands and the pan)

Steps

  1. Shell the corn
  2. Blend the kernels until you have a puree
  3. In a bowl, mix one egg and a cup milk
  4. Stir in the mix a tbsp of sugar and a tsp of salt
  5. Add the blended corn
  6. Incorporate 1 cup of Sabanero cheese and 1 cup of Chitaga cheese
  7. Grease your hands with butter and shape the batter into 4 flat and round arepas (around 1 inch thick)
  8. Heat a non-stick pan and grease it with the butter
  9. Place the arepas on the pan, and cook each side for 5 minutes
  10. During the last minute of cooking, place a quarter of a cup of Chitaga cheese over two of the arepas and cover it with the other two ones
  11. Remove from the pan and serve your freshly-made Choclo Arepas
